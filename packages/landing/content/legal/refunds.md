## Trying Trackr

Every new account gets 14 days of Pro, with no card needed. When the trial ends, your account moves to the Free plan unless you subscribe, so you're never charged by surprise.

## Cancelling

- Cancel at any time from **Billing → Manage subscription** in the app. There are no cancellation fees.
- You keep Pro until the end of the month or year you've already paid for. After that, your account moves to the Free plan and keeps all of your data.
- If you'd rather leave entirely, delete your account from **Settings → Account → Delete account**. That cancels your subscription immediately.

## Refunds

- **Didn't mean to renew?** Write to us within 14 days of the charge and we'll refund that charge in full.
- **Yearly plan:** if you cancel in the first 30 days, we'll refund the whole year on request.
- **Something went wrong on our side,** such as a duplicate charge or the service not working as described: we'll refund the affected charge.
- Otherwise, payments aren't refunded for partial periods, but your plan stays active until the end of the period.

Refunds go back to the card or payment method you paid with. Your bank may take 5 to 10 business days to show them.

## Disputes

If a charge looks wrong, please contact us first. We can usually fix it faster than a card dispute.
